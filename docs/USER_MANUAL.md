# User Manual

## For Students

1. **Register / Login** — Create an account from the login page (Student
   ID, name, email, phone, department, password), or log in if you
   already have one.
2. **Submit a complaint** — From your dashboard or "My Complaints", click
   **"+ Submit Complaint"**. Fill in a title, category, location,
   priority and description; optionally attach a photo or PDF as
   evidence.
3. **Track status** — Open any complaint to see its current status,
   assigned staff member, and a full timeline of what's happened.
4. **Comment** — Add comments on a complaint to provide more detail or
   ask about progress; staff and admins can reply.
5. **Confirm or reopen** — Once a complaint is marked **Resolved**, you
   can:
   - **Confirm Resolution** — closes the complaint.
   - **Reopen — Issue Persists** — sends it back to the staff queue with
     a note explaining why.
6. **Notifications** — The bell icon shows unread updates (assignment,
   status changes, new comments). Click a notification to jump to that
   complaint.

## For Staff

1. **Login** using your staff account (created by an Admin).
2. **View assigned complaints** on the "Assigned Complaints" page;
   search/filter/sort as needed.
3. **Update status** — Open a complaint and move it through
   Assigned → In Progress → Resolved. A resolution note is required when
   marking a complaint Resolved.
4. **Upload resolution evidence** — Attach a photo/PDF showing the fix.
5. **Comment** with the student if you need more information.
6. **Dashboard** — See your workload: pending, in progress, overdue and
   critical counts.

## For Admins

1. **Login** with the admin account.
2. **All Complaints** — View, search, filter and sort every complaint
   campus-wide.
3. **Assign / Reassign** — Open any complaint and assign it to a staff
   member from the dropdown; this also routes it to that staff member's
   department.
4. **Users & Staff** — Create new staff accounts, and activate/deactivate
   any student or staff account.
5. **Categories & Departments** — Add new complaint categories or campus
   departments as the institution's needs grow.
6. **Dashboard** — Org-wide stats: totals by status/priority/category/
   department, a monthly trend chart, overdue/critical counts, and staff
   workload.

## Common Actions

- **Change your password**: Profile page → Change Password.
- **Update your name/phone**: Profile page → Profile Details → Save.
- **Log out**: "Logout" button in the top-right of any page.
